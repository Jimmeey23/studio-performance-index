## Brand
- Physique 57 is a New York barre method (founded 2006 at 24 W 57th Street by Jennifer Vaughan Maanavi and Tanya Becker, a modern take on the Lotte Berk Method). It has run in NYC, the Hamptons, LA, Dubai, Bangkok and Mumbai.
- Physique 57 India is run by AMP Fitness LLP, the exclusive licensee, founded by Mallika Tarkas Parekh (M.S., M.P.H.). The positioning is preventative health: bone and muscle health, a low-impact but high-intensity workout, and visible results in about 8 classes.
- Tagline and voice: "Stronger. Longer. Leaner. Happier." / "Sculpting bodies. Changing lives." / "Legendary workouts for every body." The tone is premium, warm, motivating and science-backed. Never discount-led.
- The method is "Interval Overload": muscles are worked to fatigue and then stretched. It blends cardio, strength, stretching and recovery in 57 minutes, using a ballet barre, light weights, bands and body weight, with influences from functional training, HIIT, pilates and yoga.
- Trainers: about 90 days and 200 hours of training before teaching. The brand says only about 5% of applicants make the cut.
